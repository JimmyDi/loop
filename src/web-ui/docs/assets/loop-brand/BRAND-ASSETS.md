# Loop brand asset policy

## Scope

The approved Loop icon artwork is excluded from the repository's MIT license. To the extent protectable rights exist, all rights in that artwork are reserved by the applicable rights holder, except for the limited permissions below and uses allowed by law.

This policy covers:

- The icon and mark artwork in `svg/`, `png/` and `native/`.
- Icon images in `web/`, including SVG, PNG and ICO files.
- The application's copies in `src/web-ui/frontend/assets/` and their bundled output.
- Reproductions of that artwork in the presentation board, PDF guide, other documentation, and the delivery ZIP, including artwork extracted from those files.

This policy applies to the icon artwork, not to the underlying software, documentation text, code examples or manifest template, which remain under the repository's MIT license. Fonts and their glyph outlines retain their applicable SIL OFL 1.1 terms; see [font attribution](fonts/README.md) and [OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt). The fonts are not exclusive to Loop.

## Limited permissions

- Copies of the artwork may be retained unchanged in repository mirrors, source forks and complete delivery-package copies, with this policy included, for preservation and development of Loop. This does not authorize adopting the icon as the branding of a fork or another product.
- The unmodified icon may be displayed solely to identify and discuss the actual Loop project in documentation, reviews and links, without suggesting sponsorship or endorsement.
- Other reproduction, modification, distribution or use of the artwork requires separate written permission from the applicable rights holder, unless allowed by law. In particular, this policy grants no permission to use the icon as the identity of another product, service, organization or separately branded fork, or to sell or sublicense it as a stock icon.

Technical export files and usage examples explain how the artwork is constructed and deployed; they do not grant additional brand-use permission. A separately branded fork should supply its own icons while retaining any notices required for source copies.

No trademark license is granted beyond any rights needed for the limited identification uses expressly permitted above. Statutory exceptions and previously granted valid licenses are unaffected.
